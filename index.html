Privacy Policy

World Bites · Dural Games

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Game: World Bites, also identified as Cuisine Show in early test builds

Privacy contact: durallgames@gmail.com

1. ABOUT THIS POLICY

Dural Games ("we", "us", or "our") explains here how information is handled when you play World Bites or contact us. The game is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Version notice: The current 0.1.0 test build does not integrate Google AdMob or Firebase Analytics. We plan to introduce these services in a later release. The sections describing advertising and analytics apply only to releases in which those services are enabled. Before activating them, we will update this notice and provide any required privacy choices. This policy itself does not constitute consent to advertising or analytics.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The game stores progress, unlocked restaurants, in-game currency, upgrades and language preferences locally on your device so you can continue playing. The current game does not require an account and does not provide a developer-operated cloud-save service.

You can remove local game data through Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the game. This can permanently remove your progress. Device backup and restoration services, where applicable, are controlled by your device or platform settings and their providers.

The current game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise GPS location.

3. INFORMATION YOU SEND US

If you email durallgames@gmail.com, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Our email provider processes support correspondence to deliver and store our communications.

4. PLANNED ADVERTISING: GOOGLE ADMOB

In releases with advertising, we plan to use Google AdMob to display and measure ads and help prevent fraud. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, your device settings and applicable consent, Google Mobile Ads can process:

  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location;
  • advertising, app-set and other applicable device identifiers;
  • ad views, clicks and other app or advertising interactions;
  • device and app information and technical diagnostics.

Google processes this information to deliver advertising, measure performance and protect its services. Personalized advertising, when enabled and permitted, can use information about interests or previous activity. Non-personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no data is processed.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google's partner-data explanation: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. PLANNED ANALYTICS: GOOGLE ANALYTICS FOR FIREBASE

In releases with analytics, we plan to use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used and improve its balance and usability. Depending on the implemented events and settings, this can include app opens, sessions, engagement, restaurant or level progression, app-instance identifiers, app version, device characteristics, language and approximate geographic information.

Advertising identifiers and advertising-related uses depend on the configuration and applicable privacy choices. We will not intentionally include your support email, name or message contents in gameplay analytics events.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. UNITY AND GOOGLE PLAY

World Bites is built with Unity. Where runtime diagnostics are enabled, technical information such as device and operating-system details, application identifiers, errors and performance information may be processed by Unity to diagnose problems. This is distinct from the planned AdMob and Firebase integrations.

Unity's player privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Google Play also processes information when you download, update, review or test the game under Google's policies. We may receive platform reports, including aggregate installation or technical-quality statistics, and feedback that you choose to submit. These platform activities are separate from an in-game Firebase Analytics integration.

7. PURPOSES, SHARING AND INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

We use information to operate the game, preserve local progress, respond to support requests, improve the game and, in applicable releases, provide advertising and analytics.

Information may be handled by the service providers described above for their stated functions, or disclosed when reasonably necessary to meet legal obligations or protect users and the service. We do not publish your private support correspondence.

Providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Their linked policies explain their processing locations and transfer arrangements. Where applicable law requires safeguards or consent for a transfer, those requirements must be satisfied before the relevant processing begins.

Where data protection law requires a lawful basis, this may be providing the requested service, our legitimate interests in support and security, compliance with law, or consent for processing that requires it. We will not treat downloading or continuing to play as a substitute for consent where specific consent is required.

8. PRIVACY CHOICES

Before enabling advertising or analytics that requires consent, we will provide the necessary notice and consent controls. Advertising and analytics choices must cover their respective purposes; agreeing to one does not automatically authorize the other. In applicable releases, we will provide a way to revisit and withdraw choices.

Android also provides advertising-identifier controls on supported devices. Changing those settings does not necessarily stop all advertising, diagnostics or data processing, and does not automatically delete information already held by providers.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. Contact durallgames@gmail.com to make a request. We may ask for information reasonably needed to identify relevant records and verify your request. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. RETENTION, DELETION AND SECURITY

Local progress remains on your device until it is removed or overwritten. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and any related legal or dispute-resolution requirements.

In releases using advertising or analytics, provider-held information is subject to the service's retention rules and the retention settings we configure. We will select retention periods appropriate to the stated purposes and describe material changes before enabling new processing. Removing the game does not by itself delete records already held by us or a provider. Contact us for assistance with such requests; aggregated information that no longer identifies a person may be retained separately.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Local game files are not intended to store sensitive personal information.

10. CHILDREN AND TEENAGERS

The game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact durallgames@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Being 13 or older does not automatically mean a user can legally consent to all data processing. In releases with advertising or analytics, applicable additional protections for teenagers and users below their local age of consent must be respected.

11. CHANGES AND CONTACT

We may update this policy as the game or its data practices change. We will revise the date above and provide additional notice or request consent where required. A policy update does not override choices already made or authorize processing that requires new consent.
